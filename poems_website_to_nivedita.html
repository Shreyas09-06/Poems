To Nivedita

A few words before the poems begin.

Dear Nivedita, These pages hold words I wrote during a chapter of my life that we shared. Some came from happy moments, some from difficult ones, and some from feelings I did not always know how to express. I wanted to bring them together in one place—not to change anything, ask anything of you, or make you feel that you need to respond. They are simply a record of what I felt and what that time meant to me. I respect where life has brought us now, and I hope the days ahead are kind to you. Thank you for the memories that became part of these pages. Take care, — Shreyas
